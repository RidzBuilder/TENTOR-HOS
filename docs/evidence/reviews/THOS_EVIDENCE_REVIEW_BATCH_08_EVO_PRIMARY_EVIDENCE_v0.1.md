## 2. Per-artifact review

### TH-EV-015 — AOS External Reference: Multiverse Ambition & Creator Multiverse Case Study

The document is explicitly a research reference/supporting evidence/hypothesis dated 2026-09-13, not a final foundation or proof of the Multiverse AOS hypothesis. It describes the ambition of AOS as a meta-level environment that can create and manage nested Multiverses/Universes and agent ecosystems. It proposes a conceptual chain from AOS/Multiverse through Universe, agent ecosystem, agent, capability, workflow, artifact/output, outcome, evaluation, evidence, and evolution.

The Creator Multiverse case distinguishes a Store Universe, Creator Universe, and Affiliate Creator Universe, each with its own identity, boundary, state, objective, policy, assets, and lifecycle, while allowing controlled interfaces/interoperability. It distinguishes Universe from Agent and ecosystem outcome (e.g., Social Buzz) from a single agent, workflow, or artifact. It separates a Management/Control Plane from an Execution Plane, connected through state, events, evidence, decisions, and results.

The source explicitly leaves operational definitions and open questions unresolved: what qualifies as a Universe or Multiverse, how these differ from workspace/project/tenant/organization/agent group/orchestration layer, and how nesting, boundaries, authority, interfaces, communication, lifecycle, resources, and evolution work. Therefore these concepts remain hypotheses/research inputs, not accepted ontology or architecture.
