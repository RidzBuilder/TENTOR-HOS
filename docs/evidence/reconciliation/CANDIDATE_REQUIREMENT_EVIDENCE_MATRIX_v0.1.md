## 1. Rules

This matrix converts cross-source convergence candidates into falsifiable candidate requirements. It does not promote them to TENTOR HOS requirements. Source references point to registry IDs TH-EV-001–061 and reviewed batches 01–09. Because several artifacts are derivative or companion representations, repeated mentions are not counted as independent corroboration. “Evidence required” means future acceptance evidence, not evidence already obtained.
