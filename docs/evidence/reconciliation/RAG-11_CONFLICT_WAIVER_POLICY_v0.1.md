# RAG-11 — Conflict Adjudication and Waiver Policy v0.1

**Status:** WORKING CANDIDATE / NON-CANONICAL / NOT ACCEPTED

## Conflict
- Conflicting evidence remains visible and is marked CONFLICTED until authorized adjudication.
- Later timestamp does not automatically supersede earlier evidence.
- Supersession requires lineage and an applicable governance decision.
- Missing provenance remains UNKNOWN/MISSING and is not repaired by inference.

## Waiver
- A waiver is a governance decision, not a test result.
- It must identify authority, subject, scope, reason, conditions, duration/review point, and evidence references.
- It cannot falsify or relabel the underlying result.
- Mandatory security/authorization constraints cannot be silently waived.
- Expired or revoked waivers cannot satisfy current criteria.

Unresolved conflict or unauthorized waiver blocks promotion.
