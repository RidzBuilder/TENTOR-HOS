# RAG-09 — Governance Decision / Approval Record v0.1

**Status:** WORKING CANDIDATE / NON-CANONICAL / NOT ACCEPTED

The candidate record makes authority, scope, revision, decision type, evidence references, conditions, and reason explicit.

A decision record is governance evidence; it is not technical proof. The authority reference remains a placeholder until an actual governance authority is formally defined and authorized.

The record supports approval, rejection, scoped acceptance, revocation, deprecation, waiver, and retest decisions. Waiver is governance state and cannot silently convert FAIL/BLOCKED into PASS.
